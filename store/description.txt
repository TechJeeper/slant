Time cut through louvers.

Slant covers your wrist in horizontal architectural slats. A soft glow sits behind the material. Hours and minutes are punched through as cut light — readable at a glance, and unmistakably different from a flat digital face.

Presets
• Gallery Aluminum — cool gray louvers, cream daylight
• Mid-century Oak — warm wood, amber glow
• Night Blackout — charcoal slats, coral glow
• Paper White — pale louvers, ink-blue cut light
• Custom — pick slat, glow, and shadow colors

Make it yours
• Slat density: fine, medium, or bold
• Layouts: stacked hours over minutes, side-by-side, or corner editorial
• Accent strip: date, weekday, battery, or steps
• Fixed glow or a day-cycle that shifts with the hour
• Resting open amount: tight, normal, or airy
• Flick your wrist to briefly open the louvers

Battery-minded by design
• Updates once a minute
• No looping animation
• Low battery collapses to a simpler stacked cut
